## Sandy the STEM Student

Sandy is a PhD student in their first or second semester, who has previous experience writing code at undergraduate level, for their final-year project. They were recommended to take the Version Control with Git course by their Centre for Doctoral Training, and can see how the material will help them keep their code safe and help their development practises.

## Jay the Geographer

Jay is a PhD student late on in their first year, or in their second. They have noticed that their code is starting to get messy, with lots of old or duplicate files lying around, and want to learn how to improve the situation.
